About me

Put an artist, a lumberjack, and a botanist in the same forest, and they’ll each see a totally different world. UX design bridges those gaps—uncovering every perspective so we can build products that work for everyone 🤝.

With over a decade of hands-on experience in hardware, a background in software engineering, an IT & Business Systems degree, and training in psychology and NLP, I view digital systems through a uniquely multi-dimensional lens. My practice sits at the exact intersection of user psychology, technical feasibility, and business viability 🧑‍💻.

The Journey

2000–2004

The Tinkerer Roots

Discovered networking over DOS, dove into XDA Developers, and learned to navigate mobile core files, unlock network locks, and repair early Windows Mobile hardware.

2006–2007

Early Entrepreneurship (Impro Studio)

Founded a tech and web design studio. Solved early iPhone network bottlenecks in Nigeria by caching 1GB IPSW software updates locally—naturally evolving hardware fixes into full digital branding and web projects.

2009

The Pivot

Studied Psychology at the University of Lagos while auditing Harvard courses online. Realizing local curricula weren’t keeping pace with tech, I sold my possessions, finished the Harvard course and dropped out of Unilag, then self-funded a transition into Aptech software engineering.

2013

Enterprise IT & Service Design

Managed corporate Apple fleets and partnered with telecom branches (Etisalat/Glo). Solved complex device issues for their customer service teams, building a referral pipeline and solving service touchpoints on the ground.

2015

Physical UX & Upcycling

Tackled urban bottle waste by researching, cutting, and refining glass bottles into table lamps and glassware. Validated the product with local hotels and bars, building an operational pipeline before relocating to the UK.

2016

End-to-End Product Design

I graduated from Middlesex University London, formally unifying my background in human behavior, hardware, and code into modern Product/UX Design.

2026 – Present

CEO @ Inject

Directing an R&D company focused on two core pillars: taking proprietary products from concept to launch, and building mission-critical digital infrastructure for fintech and public sector clients.
